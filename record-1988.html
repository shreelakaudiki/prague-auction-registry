ARCHIVE REFERENCE · PAR-1988-073

Unidentified Crystal Jewel

Jewellery · Private Holdings

Year 1988
Location Prague
Register Autumn Auction

Description

An unusually formed jewel consisting of a central crystal surrounded by five diamonds. No confirmed maker or original catalogue title was entered in the surviving register.

Provenance

Previous ownership could not be established from the documentation accompanying the object. The item was submitted as part of a private collection.

Auction Record

The object was entered into the autumn register but was withdrawn before public bidding began. No completed sale is recorded.

Status

Withdrawn · Ownership Dispute

Registrar's Note

Ownership documentation was disputed prior to the scheduled auction. No public bidding took place and the object was removed from the active register.

Archival Instruction

The Prague catalogue noted that the jewel was removed before the final value.T
